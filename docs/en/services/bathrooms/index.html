Bathrooms

A bathroom from the ground up: tile removal, wet-area waterproofing and new tiles.

Request a quoteCalculate the price
from2.800kr / m²excl. VAT

A bathroom renovation has several stages that must fit together. We handle the finishing part: we remove the old tiles, seal the wet area and lay the new surfaces.

We waterproof with a liquid membrane and sealing tapes – this is what protects the structure from moisture.

What the service includes

  • Removing old tiles
  • Liquid waterproofing membrane with sealing tapes
  • Floor and wall tiles
  • Large formats and mosaics
  • Levelling the substrate

How we work

Frequently asked questions

Bathrooms

How much does wet-area waterproofing cost?

Applying the liquid membrane with sealing tapes costs 2.800–4.000 kr/m², depending on the scope.

Can you do the whole bathroom finish?

Yes, we do the tile removal, waterproofing and tiling, and you will find the price of each stage in the price list.

Why do wall and floor tiles have different rates?

Wall tiles take more work in cutting and levelling, which is why the rates are 9.500 and 7.500 kr/m².

Do I need to remove the old tiles beforehand?

No, we remove them ourselves. It is priced as a separate item: 6.000 kr/m².

Can I get a quote based on photos?

Yes, send photos and dimensions and we will prepare a preliminary quote. We confirm the final one after a site visit.
