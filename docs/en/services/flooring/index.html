Common questions

Flooring questions

Do I need self-levelling compound?

Only if the floor is uneven, because panels need an even base. It costs 3.000 kr/m², labour only.

What is the difference between click and glued panels?

Click panels go down without adhesive (1.800–2.300 kr/m²). Vinyl panels are glued to the floor beneath (4.000 kr/m²).

Will you take up the old panels?

Yes. We remove the old panels and prepare the surface. It costs 2.000 kr/m².

Does the shape of the room affect the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around radiator pipes take more time, so labour costs more.

How should the floor be prepared for panels?

The floor must be level and clean. If it is uneven we apply self-levelling compound, and we take up the old floor.
