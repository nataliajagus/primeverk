General

Why are prices excl. VAT?

We quote net prices so they are clear and comparable. VAT is added on the invoice, and every price on the site is marked as excl. VAT.

Are the prices in the price list final?

They are base prices. Every job is adjusted to the client's needs, so we give the final price in the offer once we know the scope of work.

Where do you work?

In Reykjavík and the surrounding area, including Keflavík and Selfoss. If you are outside this area, get in touch.

How can I request a quote?

Call, send an email, fill in the form or send the estimate from the calculator.

What languages can I contact you in?

The site is available in Icelandic, English and Polish. Write to us in the language you prefer.

Painting

Does the price include paint?

The stated rate covers labour only. We discuss materials when quoting – you can buy the paint yourself or agree to have us buy it.

How many coats of paint do you apply?

Usually two coats, but it depends on the colour, the condition of the walls and the paint used. We set out the exact scope in the offer.

What if the walls are in poor condition?

If plastering or crack repair is needed, we price it separately according to the plastering price list.

How long does it take to paint an apartment?

It depends on the area and how much the walls need preparing. We give an approximate time in the offer after the site visit.

Do you paint ceilings?

Yes, ceilings are part of the painting scope and we price them the same way as walls.

Plastering and repairs

What is the difference between skim coating a whole wall and plasterboard joints?

With joints we work only on the seams between boards: we embed the tape and apply two coats of compound (4.000 kr/m). Skim coating a whole surface is a separate item in the price list.

What does the price of plastering depend on?

On the condition of the walls, the number of coats and the smoothness required. That is why the price list gives a range of 3.500–5.000 kr/m².

When do old walls need renovation?

When the walls have cracks, loose areas, unevenness or many old layers of paint. In that case prices start at 5.000 kr/m².

Can I paint straight after plastering?

No, the compound has to dry and the surface has to be sanded. We agree the timing as part of the job.

Do you do plastering and painting together?

Yes, it is the most common combination. The work can be priced in a single offer.

Tiling

Why does large format cost more?

Tiles of 60×120 cm and mosaics take more time to cut and level precisely, which is why the rate is 14.000 kr/m².

Are the tiles and adhesive included in the price?

The price list covers labour. We discuss materials when quoting.

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removing old tiles is a separate item: 6.000 kr/m².

Does an irregular room change the price?

Yes, narrow hallways, columns and cutting around pipes increase the working time and therefore the cost of labour.

How soon can I start?

Write or call – after a site visit we will agree the date and scope in the offer.

Bathrooms

How much does wet-area waterproofing cost?

Applying the liquid membrane with sealing tapes costs 2.800–4.000 kr/m², depending on the scope.

Can you do the whole bathroom finish?

Yes, we do the tile removal, waterproofing and tiling, and you will find the price of each stage in the price list.

Why do wall and floor tiles have different rates?

Wall tiles take more work in cutting and levelling, which is why the rates are 9.500 and 7.500 kr/m².

Do I need to remove the old tiles beforehand?

No, we remove them ourselves. It is priced as a separate item: 6.000 kr/m².

Can I get a quote based on photos?

Yes, send photos and dimensions and we will prepare a preliminary quote. We confirm the final one after a site visit.

Flooring

Do I have to use self-levelling compound?

Only if the floor is uneven. Panels need an even substrate, which is why the screed costs 4.000 kr/m² with material.

What is the difference between click panels and glue-down panels?

Click panels connect without adhesive (1.800–2.300 kr/m²), while vinyl panels are glued to the substrate (4.000 kr/m²).

Will you remove the old floor?

Yes, we take up parquet, tiles or carpet and prepare the substrate – 2.000 kr/m².

Does the shape of the room affect the price?

Yes, narrow hallways, columns and cutting around radiator pipes lengthen the cutting time and increase the cost of labour.

Does the price include the panels?

The price list covers labour, except for the self-levelling compound, which is priced with material. We agree the rest in the offer.

Have walls, a bathroom or a floor that needs doing?

Describe what needs to be done. We will come back with a quote excl. VAT.
