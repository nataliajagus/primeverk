General

Why are prices excl. VAT?

We give net prices so they are easy to compare. VAT is added on the invoice.

Are the prices in the price list final?

No. They are base rates and the calculator result is an estimate. We set the final quote from the area, the condition of the substrate and the scope of work.

Where do you work?

In Reykjavík and surroundings, including Keflavík and Selfoss. If you are further away, get in touch.

How can I request a quote?

Call, email, describe your job in the form, or attach the estimate from the calculator.

What languages can I contact you in?

The site is available in Icelandic, English and Polish. Write in whichever suits you.

Painting

Does the price include paint?

No, the rate is for labour only. We agree materials when quoting: you can buy the paint yourself or agree with us that we buy it.

How many coats of paint do you apply?

It depends on the colour, the condition of the walls and the paint. We put the scope in the offer.

What if the walls are in poor condition?

We price plastering and crack repair separately, from the plastering price list.

How long does it take to paint an apartment?

It depends on the area and the condition of the walls. We give an approximate time in the offer, after the site visit.

Do you paint ceilings?

Yes. Ceilings are part of the painting scope. When you ask for a quote, give the total area of walls and ceilings.

Plastering and repairs

What is the difference between plastering a whole wall and plasterboard joints?

For joints we work only on the seams between boards. We embed the mesh tape and apply two coats of compound (4.000 kr/m). Plastering a whole surface is a separate item in the price list.

What does the price of plastering depend on?

On the condition of the walls and the scope of work. That is why the price list gives a range of 3.500–5.000 kr/m².

When do old walls need renovation?

When they have cracks, uneven areas or need scraping. Prices then start at 5.000 kr/m².

Can I paint straight after plastering?

The compound has to dry first. We agree when painting can start while we do the work.

Do you do plastering and painting together?

Yes. We can price both in a single offer.

Tiling

Why does large format cost more?

Tiles of 60×120 cm and mosaics take longer to cut and level precisely. The rate is 14.000 kr/m².

Are the tiles and adhesive included in the price?

The price list gives rates for the work. We confirm which materials are covered (tiles, adhesive and grout) in the quote.

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removing tiles is a separate item at 6.000 kr/m².

Does an irregular room change the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around pipes add working time, so labour costs more.

How soon can I start?

Write or call. We agree the date and scope after a site visit and put them in the offer.

Bathroom finishing

How much does wet-area waterproofing cost?

The liquid membrane with sealing tapes costs 2.800–4.000 kr/m² (excl. VAT), depending on the scope. This is the rate for waterproofing only, not for the whole bathroom.

What does bathroom finishing include?

Removing old tiles, preparing the substrate, waterproofing the wet area and laying new tiles. The rates for each type of work are in the price list.

How much do wall and floor tiles cost?

Floor tiles cost 7.500 kr/m², wall tiles 9.500 kr/m², and large formats or mosaics 14.000 kr/m².

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removing tiles is a separate item at 6.000 kr/m².

Can I get a quote based on photos?

Send photos and dimensions and we will prepare a preliminary quote. We confirm the final one after a site visit.

Flooring

Do I have to use self-levelling compound?

Only if the floor is uneven. Panels need an even substrate. Self-levelling compound costs 4.000 kr/m², material included.

What is the difference between click panels and glued panels?

Click panels are laid without adhesive (1.800–2.300 kr/m²). Vinyl panels are glued to the substrate (4.000 kr/m²).

Will you remove the old floor?

Yes. We take up parquet, tiles or carpet and prepare the substrate. It costs 2.000 kr/m².

Does the shape of the room affect the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around radiator pipes mean more cutting time, so labour costs more.

Does the price include the panels?

The price list covers the laying work. Material is included only in the self-levelling compound. We confirm the other materials in the quote.
