General

Why are prices excl. VAT?

We quote net prices so they are easy to compare. VAT is added on the invoice.

Are the listed prices final?

No. They are base rates, and the calculator gives an estimate. The final quote depends on the area, the state of the surface and what needs doing.

Where do you work?

In Reykjavík and nearby, including Keflavík and Selfoss. Live further out? Get in touch.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call, email, describe your job in the form, or attach the estimate from the calculator.

Which languages can I use?

The site is in Icelandic, English and Polish. Write in whichever suits you.

Painting

Is paint included in the price?

No, the rate is for labour only. We settle materials when we quote. You can buy the paint yourself or ask us to.

How many coats do you apply?

That depends on the colour, the walls and the paint. We write it into the offer.

What if the walls are in poor condition?

Plastering and crack repair are priced separately, from the plastering rates.

How long does it take to paint an apartment?

It depends on the area and the state of the walls. After the site visit, the offer gives an approximate time.

Do you paint ceilings?

Yes, ceilings are part of the job. When you ask for a quote, give the total area of walls and ceilings.

Plastering and repairs

How is plastering a whole wall different from plasterboard joints?

For joints we work only on the seams between boards. We embed mesh tape and apply two coats of compound (4.000 kr/m). Plastering a whole surface is a separate line in the price list.

What decides the price of plastering?

The state of the walls and how much needs doing. That is why the price list gives a range of 3.500–5.000 kr/m².

When do old walls need renovating?

When they are cracked, uneven or need scraping. Prices then start at 5.000 kr/m².

Can I paint straight after plastering?

Not quite. The compound has to dry first. We agree when painting can begin as the work goes on.

Can you plaster and paint in one job?

Yes. We can put both in a single offer.

Tiling

Why does large format cost more?

Tiles of 60×120 cm and mosaics take longer to cut and level precisely. The rate is 14.000 kr/m².

Are tiles and adhesive included?

No. The rates cover labour only. Tiles, adhesive and grout are settled in the quote.

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removal is a separate line at 6.000 kr/m².

Does an awkward room change the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around pipes add time, so labour costs more.

How soon can I start?

Write or call. We agree the date and the work after a site visit, then put both in the offer.

Bathroom finishing

How much does wet-area waterproofing cost?

The liquid membrane with sealing tapes costs 2.800–4.000 kr/m² (excl. VAT), depending on the job. That is the rate for waterproofing only, not for the whole bathroom.

What does bathroom finishing cover?

Removing old tiles, preparing the surface, waterproofing the wet area and laying new tiles. Each type of work has its rate in the price list.

How much do wall and floor tiles cost?

Floor tiles cost 7.500 kr/m², wall tiles 9.500 kr/m², and large formats or mosaics 14.000 kr/m².

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removal is a separate line at 6.000 kr/m².

Can I get a quote from photos?

Send photos and measurements and we will prepare a first quote. We confirm the final one after a site visit.

Flooring

Do I need self-levelling compound?

Only if the floor is uneven, because panels need an even base. It costs 4.000 kr/m², labour only.

What is the difference between click and glued panels?

Click panels go down without adhesive (1.800–2.300 kr/m²). Vinyl panels are glued to the floor beneath (4.000 kr/m²).

Will you take up the old floor?

Yes. We remove parquet, tiles or carpet and prepare the surface. It costs 2.000 kr/m².

Does the shape of the room affect the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around radiator pipes take more time, so labour costs more.

Are the panels included in the price?

No. The price list covers labour only, without materials. Materials, including the panels, are settled in the quote.
