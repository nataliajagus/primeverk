General

Why are prices excl. VAT?

Net prices are clear and easy to compare. VAT is added on the invoice, and every price on the site is marked excl. VAT.

Are the prices in the price list final?

They are base prices. We adjust every job to what the client needs, so the final price is in the offer, once we know the scope of work.

Where do you work?

In Reykjavík and the surrounding area, including Keflavík and Selfoss. If you are outside this area, get in touch.

How can I request a quote?

Call, email, fill in the form or send the estimate from the calculator.

What languages can I contact you in?

The site is available in Icelandic, English and Polish. Write to us in whichever you prefer.

Painting

Does the price include paint?

No. The rate covers labour only. We agree materials when quoting: you can buy the paint yourself or ask us to buy it.

How many coats of paint do you apply?

Usually two. It depends on the colour, the condition of the walls and the paint. The offer states the exact scope.

What if the walls are in poor condition?

If they need plastering or crack repair, we price that separately from the plastering price list.

How long does it take to paint an apartment?

It depends on the area and how much preparation the walls need. After the site visit, the offer gives an approximate time.

Do you paint ceilings?

Yes. Ceilings are part of the painting scope and we price them like walls.

Plastering and repairs

What is the difference between skim coating a whole wall and plasterboard joints?

For joints we work only on the seams between boards. We embed the mesh tape and apply two coats of compound (4.000 kr/m). Skim coating a whole surface is a separate item in the price list.

What does the price of plastering depend on?

On the condition of the walls, the number of coats and how smooth the finish must be. That is why the price list gives a range of 3.500–5.000 kr/m².

When do old walls need renovation?

When they have cracks, loose areas, uneven patches or many old layers of paint. Prices then start at 5.000 kr/m².

Can I paint straight after plastering?

No. The compound has to dry and the surface has to be sanded first. We agree the timing as part of the job.

Do you do plastering and painting together?

Yes, it is the most common combination. We can price both in a single offer.

Tiling

Why does large format cost more?

Tiles of 60×120 cm and mosaics take longer to cut and level accurately, so the rate is 14.000 kr/m².

Are the tiles and adhesive included in the price?

No. The price list covers labour. We agree materials when quoting.

Do you remove old tiles?

Yes. Removing old tiles is a separate item at 6.000 kr/m².

Does an irregular room change the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around pipes add working time, so labour costs more.

How soon can I start?

Write or call. After a site visit we agree the date and scope in the offer.

Bathrooms

How much does wet-area waterproofing cost?

Applying the liquid membrane with sealing tapes costs 2.800–4.000 kr/m², depending on the scope.

Can you do the whole bathroom finish?

Yes. We do the tile removal, waterproofing and tiling, and the price list shows the cost of each stage.

Why do wall and floor tiles have different rates?

Wall tiles take more work to cut and level, so the rates are 9.500 and 7.500 kr/m².

Do I need to remove the old tiles beforehand?

No, we remove them ourselves. It is a separate item at 6.000 kr/m².

Can I get a quote based on photos?

Yes. Send photos and dimensions and we will prepare a preliminary quote. We confirm the final price after a site visit.

Flooring

Do I have to use self-levelling compound?

Only if the floor is uneven. Panels need an even substrate. The screed costs 4.000 kr/m², material included.

What is the difference between click panels and glue-down panels?

Click panels lock together without adhesive (1.800–2.300 kr/m²). Vinyl panels are glued to the substrate (4.000 kr/m²).

Will you remove the old floor?

Yes. We take up parquet, tiles or carpet and prepare the substrate for 2.000 kr/m².

Does the shape of the room affect the price?

Yes. Narrow hallways, columns and cutting around radiator pipes mean more cutting time, so labour costs more.

Does the price include the panels?

No. The price list covers labour, except for the self-levelling compound, which is priced with material. We agree the rest in the offer.

Walls, a bathroom or a floor that needs doing?

Tell us what needs doing and we will send you a quote excl. VAT.
