FAQ
Why are prices excl. VAT?

We quote net prices so they are clear and comparable. VAT is added on the invoice, and every price on the site is marked as excl. VAT.

Are the prices in the price list final?

They are base prices. Every job is adjusted to the client's needs, so we give the final price in the offer once we know the scope of work.

Where do you work?

In Reykjavík and the surrounding area, including Keflavík and Selfoss. If you are outside this area, get in touch.

How can I request a quote?

Call, send an email, fill in the form or send the estimate from the calculator.

Have walls, a bathroom or a floor that needs doing?

Describe what needs to be done. We will come back with a quote excl. VAT.
